⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTES

  • NR means Not Reported. Do not convert NR to zero.
  • Values from different standards, frequencies, temperatures, humidity and specimen geometries should not be directly ranked.
  • Electrical caution: applied voltage and leakage current are frequently absent from natural-fiber composite papers. Breakdown strength, dielectric constant and resistance are different measurements.
  • Patent caution: patent examples describe claimed/prepared formulations; they are not automatically validated as high-voltage insulators.
  • Best use: use the Literature Database as the baseline. Enter your own specimen measurements in "Your Test Data", then compare matched conditions.
